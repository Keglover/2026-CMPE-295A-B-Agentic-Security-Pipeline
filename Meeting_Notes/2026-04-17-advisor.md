### **Risk Scoring System Discussion**

#### **Risk Engine Structure**

* Risk engine categorizes attacks into multiple classes including:  
  * Exfiltration  
  * Instruction override  
  * Tool coercion  
  * Prompt injection  
* Rules currently rely primarily on regex/pattern-based detection

#### **Rule Development**

* Team currently has 28+ implemented rules  
* Rules were derived from:  
  * Academic papers  
  * OWASP Top 10  
  * InjectAgent research  
  * Confused Deputy Problem research  
  * Additional prompt injection/security studies  
* Documentation references may need updating due to additional rules added recently

#### **Future Improvements**

* Team acknowledged limitations of regex-only detection  
* ML/LLM judge intended to improve contextual detection capabilities  
* Long prompt and advanced adversarial testing still required
